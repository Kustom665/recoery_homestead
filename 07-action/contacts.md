# Contacts

Who to actually call. Verify current numbers and application windows — programs
change, deadlines move.

---

## Call these first

### Your county Single County Authority (SCA)

The local drug and alcohol commission. **The single most important relationship
in this project** — they control referrals, and referrals determine whether your
beds fill.

- **Berks County:** Council on Chemical Abuse — [cocaberks.org](https://cocaberks.org/)
- Every PA county has one. Find yours through DDAP's county listing.

Ask: *"What are you short of? Where do you have nowhere to send people?"*

### County opioid settlement coordinator

Recovery housing is an explicitly eligible use, and these programs frequently
have more money than credible applicants.

- **Montgomery County** — [Competitive Opioid Remediation Application](https://www.montgomerycountypa.gov/departments/office-innovation-strategy-performance/opioid-settlements/competitive-opioid-remediation-application) ($59.7M)
- **Bucks County** — [Opioid Settlement Funds Overview](https://www.buckscounty.gov/2229/OSF-Overview)
- **Berks County** — via [Council on Chemical Abuse](https://cocaberks.org/berks-opioid-settlement-funding-request-process/)
- **Delaware County** — [Opioid Settlement](https://www.delcopa.gov/opioid-settlement)
- **Dauphin County** — [Opioid Remediation Grant Program](https://www.dauphincounty.gov/government/human-services/opioid-remediation-grant-program-to-award-settlement-funds)
- **York County** — [via York County Planning Commission](https://www.ycpc.org/646/Opioid-Settlement-Funding)
- **Statewide oversight** — [PA Opioid Trust](https://www.paopioidtrust.org/payment-spending-and-reporting)

Whatever county you choose will have one. Find its coordinator.

### PA DDAP — Licensing

Call before you buy land. Ask what goes wrong in new applications.

- [Apply to Become a Licensed Recovery House](https://www.pa.gov/services/ddap/apply-to-become-a-licensed-recovery-house)
- [DDAP Licensing — for professionals](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/ddap/for-professionals/licensing)
- [28 Pa. Code Part V — DDAP regulations](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=%2Fsecure%2Fpacode%2Fdata%2F028%2FpartVtoc.html&d=reduce)

## Funding

### USDA Rural Development — Pennsylvania State Office

The 40-year, no-fee, no-prepayment-penalty loan for essential community
facilities. Transitional housing is explicitly eligible.

- [USDA RD Pennsylvania](https://www.rd.usda.gov/pa)
- [Community Facilities Direct Loan & Grant — PA](https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/community-facilities/community-facilities-direct-loan-grant-program-13)
- [Community Facilities Programs — national](https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/community-facilities)

### PHFA — PHARE

- [PHARE program overview](https://www.phfa.org/legislation/act105.aspx)
- [PHARE application portal](https://phare.phfa.org/funding/)

Deadline has historically been around November 20 at 2:00 PM. **Confirm the
current cycle directly.**

### Others

- **SAMHSA** — federal recovery housing and recovery community organization grants
- **CDBG** — through your county community development office
- **HUD Continuum of Care** — through your regional CoC

## Land and site

### Municipal Sewage Enforcement Officer (SEO)

Get the name and number from the township. Perc tests and soil profiles run
through this person. One of the two or three most important people in the
project.

### County Conservation District

Erosion and sedimentation control, stormwater, agricultural resources.

### County Planning Commission

Zoning status, SALDO, subdivision and land development requirements.

### Township secretary

Ask, and get in writing:
1. Does the township have a zoning ordinance?
2. Does it have a subdivision and land development ordinance?
3. Are there any other ordinances regulating construction or occupancy?

### PA DEP

- [Act 537 Sewage Facilities Program](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dep/programs-and-services/water/clean-water/wastewater-management/act-537-sewage-facilities-program)
- Regional office for your county — geology mapping, planning modules

### PennDOT

Only if your parcel fronts a state road — Highway Occupancy Permit. **Prefer a
township road and skip this entirely.**

## Standards and peers

### NARR / PA affiliate

National Alliance for Recovery Residences — certification and operational
standards. Your model maps to roughly Level II or III.

### Jacob's Ladder at Brookside Farm

Aurora, WV. The program in *Recovery Boys*.

- [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/JacobsLadderBrookside/)
- [Program overview](https://recovery.com/jacobs-ladder-aurora-west-virginia/)

**Worth a call and a visit.** People running programs like this are generally
generous with their time, and an afternoon walking their property will teach you
more than a month of reading. Ask what they would do differently.

| Role | When | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PA land-use / nonprofit attorney | **Week 1** | Most important hire. Ask if they've done group home zoning. |
| PA-licensed architect or engineer | Before permits | Community building will need a stamp |
| Septic system designer | Before the site plan is final | Flow calculations drive phasing |
| Surveyor | After closing | |
| Well driller | Phase 0 | Ask about neighboring well depths first |
| Insurance broker | Before opening | Find one who has written recovery housing |
